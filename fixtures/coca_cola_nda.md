MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made between The Coca-Cola Company ("Disclosing Party") and Sellist Advisory LLP ("Receiving Party") in connection with Project Fizz.

1. Definitions. "Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party, including the documents filed under matter 0001/10001, financial models, integration plans and anything marked "Confidential" or "Project Fizz". "Representatives" means the Receiving Party's employees and engaged agents who have a need to know for the Purpose. "Purpose" means evaluating integration risk for the proposed transaction between the parties.

2. Permitted Disclosure. The Receiving Party shall disclose Confidential Information only to its Representatives who have a need to know for the Purpose, and shall not disclose Confidential Information to any other person, including any competitor of the Disclosing Party such as PepsiCo.

3. Use Restriction. The Receiving Party shall use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose and for no other purpose, including benchmarking, marketing or any work for another client.

4. Third Parties. The Receiving Party shall not transmit, email or otherwise send Confidential Information to any third party or outside the Receiving Party's systems without the prior written consent of the Disclosing Party, subject to Section 6.

5. Personal Data. To the extent Confidential Information contains personal data of the Disclosing Party's employees or customers, the Receiving Party shall not reproduce such personal data in any report or communication.

6. Exceptions. The obligations in Sections 2 to 5 do not apply to information that is publicly available through no fault of the Receiving Party, or that is required to be disclosed by law, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt notice.

7. Term. This Agreement remains in force for three years from the date of signature. The obligations of confidentiality survive termination for a further two years.

8. Return or Destruction. Upon written request the Receiving Party shall return or destroy all Confidential Information within thirty days.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.
